# Getting Started

Welcome to **audiTrack** — the audit findings tracker.

This manual explains how to use the application. Use the sidebar on the left to navigate between topics.

## What is audiTrack?

audiTrack helps audit teams track findings, manage remediation work, and report progress across multiple clients — all in one place.

## Core concepts

### Clients & locations

Everything starts with a **client**. Each client has one or more **locations** — these can be physical sites, departments, or business units. You manage these in the Clients section.

### Control catalog

The **control catalog** is your library of security or compliance requirements. You define controls once here and reuse them across clients.

### Controls

**Controls** are instances of catalog items assigned to a client. They track whether a requirement is being met. The Controls section shows a full overview with progress indicators.

### Findings

A **finding** is raised when a control is not met at a location. Each finding has:

- A title and description
- A **severity**: low / medium / high / critical
- A **status**: to do / in progress / done
- An optional assignee and due date

### Remediation plans

One or more findings can be grouped into a **remediation plan**. Plans have an owner, a due date, and an effort estimate. Inside a plan you can link findings, write a description, post comments, upload evidence, and link solutions.

### Solutions

The **Solutions** section is a knowledge base of remediation approaches. Link solutions to controls or plans to avoid writing the same advice twice.

### Notes

Use **Notes** as a scratchpad. The AI assistant can convert notes into structured findings or plan descriptions.
